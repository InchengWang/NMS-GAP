## Main conclusion

The literature has strong NMS models, simulated muscle-driven agents and physical assistive loops, but these strengths rarely coexist. The closest integrated systems remain short-session and small-patient studies; none reaches longitudinal clinical co-adaptation with explicit uncertainty and safety.
