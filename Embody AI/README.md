# Embody AI

Workspace for human neuromusculoskeletal Embodied-AI literature, benchmarks, methods and future proposals.

## Contents

- [`01_core_paper_critiques/`](01_core_paper_critiques/): Embodied-AI-centered critical assessment of the 49 core papers from the first literature map.
- [`02_gap_validation/`](02_gap_validation/): counter-evidence validation of ten candidate scientific, engineering, clinical, robotics-control and sensor-substitution gaps.
- [`03_phd_proposal/`](03_phd_proposal/): advisor-facing PhD plan centered on patient NMS state, embodied control, safe interaction and longitudinal co-adaptation.

This directory intentionally distinguishes generic muscle-driven simulation, human-grounded body modeling and patient-specific physical interaction. A simulator or policy may be genuine Embodied AI without being a patient model; a detailed NMS model may be human-specific without constituting an embodied agent.
