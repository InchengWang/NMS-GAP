# PhD Proposal — Human Neuromusculoskeletal Embodied AI

This directory turns the validated Embodied-AI gaps into an advisor-facing doctoral research plan. The proposal is centered on the loop

`human NMS state → embodied control → physical interaction → state update`,

not on IMU gait reconstruction and not on a digital-twin label.

## Files

- [`PHD_PROPOSAL.md`](PHD_PROPOSAL.md): full proposal, Specific Aims 1–3, methodology, baselines, metrics, statistical analysis, risks and timeline.
- [`NOVELTY_AND_NEAREST_WORK.md`](NOVELTY_AND_NEAREST_WORK.md): claim-by-claim nearest-prior-work boundaries and prohibited overstatements.
- [`PROPOSAL_SUMMARY_ZH.md`](PROPOSAL_SUMMARY_ZH.md): concise Chinese rationale, Aim structure and laboratory-fit summary.

## Thesis spine

1. infer an identifiable, action-sufficient patient NMS state;
2. use uncertainty to govern safe embodied assistance under sensing and model shift;
3. test fast/slow patient–controller co-adaptation across repeated sessions.

The only validated `confirmed-open` core is the narrow longitudinal joint claim in EAI-G03. EAI-G01, G02, G04, G05, G08 and G09 are used only through their `partially-addressed` residuals. EAI-G06 and G07 are explicitly excluded as primary novelty claims.

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-09-01. Re-run the nearest-work search before an application, ethics submission, grant or paper novelty statement.
